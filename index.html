Graduate Tracking Lifecycle

Complete end-to-end pipeline: alumni updates → validation → tracking records → admin monitoring → report generation. Automated SMS reminders activate after 6 months of inactivity.

1

Alumni Logs In

Accesses the system and updates employment or education status.

2

System Validates & Saves

Inputs are checked and the `lastUpdated` timestamp is refreshed.

3

Grad Records Updated

Placement logs, employment stats, and profile freshness are refreshed.

4

Admin Monitors KPIs

Analytics dashboard tracks employment rate, field alignment, and freshness.

5

Reports Generated

Compliance-ready graduate tracking reports exported for accreditation.

Automated SMS Reminder Loop

If alumni does NOT update for >6 months
🔍 System detects stale profile 📲 Automatic SMS reminder sent ✉️ Alumni receives reminder 👤 Alumni logs in & updates ✅ Record validated & refreshed 📊 Tracking updated
Example SMS: "ST. AGNES ACADEMY OF CALOOCAN: Dear Alumni, please update your employment or education status through the Alumni Management System. Your response helps the school improve its graduate tracking program. Thank you."
